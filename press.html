About Glory & Glitch

Glory & Glitch is an independent YouTube channel of true, sourced stories about how gaming empires rise, fall and glitch: the consoles that changed everything, the flops that nearly sank giants, and the comebacks nobody saw coming. Nintendo, Sega, Xbox, PlayStation, Rockstar and Atari, told in full episodes and short vertical Shorts. Our original Business Empire Files (Nokia, Kodak, Enron, Pan Am and more) stay in the archive. Glory & Glitch was founded in 2026 by M J.
